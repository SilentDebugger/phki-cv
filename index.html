I'm in the third year of a BA in Japanology. I love how a language carries a whole way of thinking, and I spend a lot of my time reading, translating little things for fun, and explaining grammar to people who swear they'll never get it (they always do).

How I got into tech.

Honestly? Video games and stubbornness. I wanted to play titles that never got translated, so I taught myself enough to patch and tinker, and eventually enough Japanese to not need the patch. Fixing a bricked console the night before a trip taught me more about patience than any manual.

Since then I've set up a little home server for my family's photos, rebuilt a few laptops from parts, and quietly become the unofficial help desk of my student circle. I'm not a computer scientist — I'm the person who enjoys the moment someone stops panicking because their files are back.
