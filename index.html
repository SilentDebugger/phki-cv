A little about me.

I'm a hobby artist who paints landscapes, quiet interiors, and playful abstractions. My creative practice began with a secondhand watercolor set and slowly grew into a ritual of sketching, experimenting, and sharing work with friends.

Outside the studio, I love wandering through galleries, collecting old postcards, exploring local cafés, and learning new creative techniques.
